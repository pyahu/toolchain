# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy,
respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking
responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insulting comments,
personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information
without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and communication that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct and
will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). When no other private contact has been arranged, use the
repository's [private reporting channel](https://github.com/pyahu/toolchain/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with `Code of Conduct`. Reports will be handled confidentially, promptly,
and without retaliation. The maintainer will avoid conflicts of interest; if the report concerns
the sole maintainer, the reporter may request a mutually agreed independent community reviewer.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's four-step ladder: correction, warning, temporary
ban, and permanent ban, with consequences proportional to impact and pattern of behavior.
